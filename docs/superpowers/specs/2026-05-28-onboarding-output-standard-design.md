# Chuan dau ra onboarding theo vi tri de dam bao chat luong dau ra

Ngay: `2026-05-28`
Pham vi: onboarding van hanh cho `nhan vien moi`, `buddy`, `quan ly`
Trang thai: `draft-for-review`

## 1. Muc tieu

Tai lieu nay chot `chuan dau ra` cho onboarding cua Homies theo huong:

- onboarding theo `chang ngan`
- tach ro `vi tri chinh`
- moi chang co `3 lop kiem`
- quyet dinh qua chang dua tren `dat chuan thuc te`, khong dua tren `du so ngay`

Muc tieu cuoi cung khong phai la "da hoc xong checklist".
Muc tieu cuoi cung la de quan ly tra loi ro 5 cau hoi:

- nhan su nay dang dung duoc `vi tri nao`
- dang o muc `can kem` hay `tu lam duoc`
- diem yeu lon nhat nam o dau
- co du an toan de giao `ca co ban` chua
- neu chua dat thi can kem lai `dung muc nao`

Neu he thong khong tra loi duoc 5 cau hoi tren, onboarding chua dat muc tieu chat luong dau ra.

## 2. Co so tham chieu de chot huong

Tai lieu nay khong sao chep quy trinh noi bo cua chuoi khac.
No chi rut mau chung tu cach cac chuoi cung mo hinh cong khai ve viec:

- dao tao theo `vi tri tai cua hang`
- dung `ca that` de danh gia thay vi chi thi ly thuyet
- dung `quan ly gate` o cuoi moc quan trong

Tham chieu cong khai da xem:

- `Highlands Coffee`: mo ta barista va crew leader cho thay van hanh dua tren vi tri, thao tac, mo/dong ca, phoi hop va nang luc thuc chien
- `Gong Cha Vietnam`: nhan manh dao tao pha che, tac phong, ve sinh, va co bai kiem tra de vao trang thai chinh thuc
- `The Coffee House`: nhan manh dao tao ky nang pha che, thu ngan, va muc tieu sau huan luyen la lam duoc viec tai cua hang
- `ToCoToCo`: nhan manh doi ngu dao tao va he thong van hanh chuan cho mo hinh chuoi

Ket luan rut ra cho Homies:

- khong nen lam checklist theo ngay cung
- khong nen de 1 checklist phang dung chung cho moi vi tri
- nen dung `chang van hanh + tieu chuan vi tri + kiem thuc chien`

## 3. Huong da chot

Huong duoc chon la:

`Chang ngan + vi tri rieng + kiem 3 lop`

Cu the:

1. co `1 chang chung` cho moi nguoi moi
2. sau do vao `track vi tri chinh`
3. tiep theo la `ca that co giam sat`
4. cuoi cung la `tu chay ca co ban`

Hai vi tri chinh can tach ro:

- `thu ngan / phuc vu`
- `pha che`

Ly do phai tach:

- `thu ngan / phuc vu` dat chuan o `dung order, dung giao tiep, dung luong phuc vu`
- `pha che` dat chuan o `dung cong thuc, dung thao tac, dung nhip ra mon`

Neu khong tach, quan ly se thay mo:

- nguoi noi chuyen tot nhung pha chua dat
- nguoi pha duoc nhung nhan order chua on
- buddy cham lech vi khong co chuan rieng

## 4. Nguyen tac van hanh loi

### 4.1. Ngay chi la moc goi y

Ngay dung de:

- nhac viec
- bao cham
- so sanh tien do giua nguoi moi

Ngay khong dung de:

- tu dong cho qua chang
- suy ra "da on" chi vi du so ca

### 4.2. Moi chang phai gon

Moi chang chi nen co:

- `5-8 item bat buoc`
- `0-4 item nen lam`

Khong nen de qua nhieu item, vi se quay ve checklist phang va de tick cho xong.

### 4.3. Moi item phai la viec that

Item tot phai la:

- co the quan sat trong ca
- co tieu chuan dat ro
- co the chi ra ai cham

Khong dung item mo ho:

- "thai do tot"
- "co co gang"
- "lam on"

### 4.4. Moi item phai co 3 lop nhin

Moi item onboarding phai hien du:

- `nhan vien phai lam gi`
- `buddy quan sat gi`
- `quan ly can kiem gi`
- `tieu chuan dat la gi`

## 5. Cau truc chang de xuat

### 5.1. Chang 1 - Nen chung cho tat ca

Ap dung cho:

- moi nhan vien moi, bat ke vi tri chinh la gi

Muc tieu chang:

- vao cua hang dung ne nep
- biet nguoi ho tro
- biet luong thong tin
- biet ky luat va ve sinh nen

### 5.2. Chang 2A - Track thu ngan / phuc vu

Ap dung cho:

- nhan vien moi co vi tri chinh la thu ngan, order, phuc vu quay, ho tro khach

Muc tieu chang:

- dung duoc dau quay phia khach
- nhan order dung
- chuyen mon dung
- giu nhip tiep khach co ban

### 5.3. Chang 2B - Track pha che

Ap dung cho:

- nhan vien moi co vi tri chinh la pha che

Muc tieu chang:

- dung duoc dau quay phia san xuat
- doc dung ticket
- lam dung mon nen
- giu sach va giu nhip ra mon co ban

### 5.4. Chang 3 - Ca that co giam sat

Ap dung cho:

- moi track vi tri sau khi qua chang 2

Muc tieu chang:

- dung vi tri chinh trong ca that
- biet phoi hop voi dau quay con lai
- biet xu ly loi nho va bao loi dung luc

### 5.5. Chang 4 - Tu chay ca co ban

Ap dung cho:

- moi track vi tri sau khi qua chang 3

Muc tieu chang:

- tu vao guong
- tu xu ly luong viec co ban
- tu biet luc nao can goi ho tro

## 6. Ba lop kiem cho moi chang

### 6.1. Lop 1 - Nhan vien tu danh gia

Muc dich:

- lo diem mu
- lo muc tu tin that
- giup buddy khong doan tam ly cua nguoi moi

Moi chang can co 3 cau tu nhin lai:

- hom nay em tu tin nhat muc nao
- muc nao em van can nguoi kem sat
- neu ngay mai dung ca that, em so nhat dieu gi

Tu danh gia khong dung de cho qua chang.
No dung de huong dan kem dung diem.

### 6.2. Lop 2 - Buddy danh gia trong ca that

Day la lop quan trong nhat.

Buddy phai cham dua tren:

- quan sat truc tiep
- vi du thuc te trong ca that
- loi lap lai
- kha nang nghe nhac va sua nhanh

Buddy khong duoc cham theo cam giac mo ho.

### 6.3. Lop 3 - Quan ly duyet gate

Quan ly khong can cham moi item nho.
Quan ly chi can ra ket luan o cac gate lon:

- co duoc qua chang chua
- co duoc dung vi tri co giam sat chua
- co duoc tu chay ca co ban chua

Quan ly can thay:

- tong ket tu danh gia
- ket qua buddy
- item nao dang do
- loi do nao chua xu ly xong

## 7. Thang ket qua va rule pass chang

### 7.1. Thang ket qua cho tung item

Moi item nen co 4 ket qua de de cham va de doc:

- `chua_dat`
- `dat_khi_co_kem`
- `dat_tu_lam`
- `can_kem_lai`

Y nghia:

- `chua_dat`: chua lam duoc, chua on de giao viec
- `dat_khi_co_kem`: lam duoc neu buddy dung gan
- `dat_tu_lam`: lam duoc on dinh trong ngu canh co ban
- `can_kem_lai`: da duoc giao thu nhung bi tra ve vi loi lap lai hoac loi do

### 7.2. Trang thai quy trinh

Ngoai ket qua chat luong, item van can trang thai quy trinh:

- `chua_lam`
- `dang_hoc`
- `cho_buddy_danh_gia`
- `cho_quan_ly_duyet`
- `hoan_tat`
- `khong_ap_dung`

### 7.3. Rule qua chang

Mot chang chi duoc xem la pass khi:

- tat ca item `bat buoc` dat toi thieu `dat_khi_co_kem` hoac `dat_tu_lam` theo rule chang
- khong con `loi do`
- buddy da gui xac nhan chang
- neu chang co gate quan trong thi quan ly da duyet

### 7.4. Muc tieu dau ra theo chang

Muc tieu cuoi chang 1:

- item bat buoc dat toi thieu `dat_khi_co_kem`

Muc tieu cuoi chang 2:

- item bat buoc vi tri dat toi thieu `dat_khi_co_kem`

Muc tieu cuoi chang 3:

- phan lon item bat buoc dat `dat_tu_lam`
- nhung item nhay cam van co the o muc `dat_khi_co_kem`

Muc tieu cuoi chang 4:

- toan bo item gate phai dat `dat_tu_lam`

### 7.5. Loi do khong cho qua chang

Loi do la loi chan pass chang du nhieu item khac dep.

Danh sach loi do toi thieu:

- sai order lap lai
- sai cong thuc nen lap lai
- bo buoc ve sinh quan trong
- khong bao loi khi co su co
- de khach cho vi giao tiep hoac ban giao sai
- bo vi tri hoac mat ky luat ca
- thao tac gay rui ro an toan thuc pham

## 8. Chuan item chi tiet - Chang 1 nen chung

Chang nay ap dung cho ca `thu ngan / phuc vu` va `pha che`.

### 8.1. Item C1-01 - Biet buddy va luong ho tro

- Muc dich: nhan vien moi khong bi tha noi trong ca dau
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: nho ten buddy, biet ca nao buddy kem, biet khi bi thi hoi ai truoc
- Tu danh gia: em co nho ro nguoi kem va cach xin ho tro khong
- Buddy quan sat gi: nhan vien co hoi dung nguoi khi gap vuong hay im lang
- Quan ly can kiem gi: ca dau co nguoi kem ro, khong de nguoi moi tu boi roi
- Chua dat: khong nho buddy la ai, khong biet hoi ai
- Dat khi co kem: nho buddy, hoi duoc khi duoc nhac
- Dat tu lam: chu dong tim dung nguoi ho tro khi gap vuong
- Loi do: bi ket nhung khong hoi ai, tu xu ly sai lien tuc

### 8.2. Item C1-02 - Hieu noi quy va ky luat ca nen

- Muc dich: tranh vi pham co ban ngay tu tuan dau
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: doc noi quy co ban ve dong phuc, di tre, dien thoai, ve sinh, thai do va ban giao
- Tu danh gia: muc nao em chua ro nhat trong noi quy
- Buddy quan sat gi: nhan vien co hieu tinh than quy tac khong, hay chi bam xac nhan
- Quan ly can kiem gi: diem cam va diem de vi pham da duoc hieu dung
- Chua dat: khong nho diem co ban, tra loi mo ho
- Dat khi co kem: nho duoc diem chinh khi duoc goi y
- Dat tu lam: neu lai duoc cac diem de vi pham va ap dung dung trong ca
- Loi do: vi pham ky luat nen ngay trong chang 1

### 8.3. Item C1-03 - Nhan biet khu vuc cua hang

- Muc dich: khong mat nhip vi khong biet do nam dau
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: biet khu quay order, khu pha che, kho, tu lanh, khu rua, thung rac, cho de dung cu
- Tu danh gia: em co tu lay duoc do co ban khong
- Buddy quan sat gi: nhan vien co lay dung cho, tra dung cho
- Quan ly can kiem gi: da duoc gioi thieu khu vuc co he thong
- Chua dat: hoi lai lien tuc nhung diem rat co ban
- Dat khi co kem: lay dung do khi buddy nhac
- Dat tu lam: tu lay duoc do co ban dung cho, dung nhom
- Loi do: lay nham khu vuc gay roi quay hoac mat an toan

### 8.4. Item C1-04 - Biet vao ca, nhan ban giao, doc thong tin ca

- Muc dich: vao ca khong bi dong
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: biet xem lich, biet doc thong bao ca, biet nghe ban giao dau ca
- Tu danh gia: em vao ca da biet hom nay em dung dau chua
- Buddy quan sat gi: nhan vien co vao ca voi y thuc ro viec hay bi dong cho
- Quan ly can kiem gi: thong tin ca toi dung nguoi
- Chua dat: vao ca nhung khong biet minh phai lam gi
- Dat khi co kem: hieu viec sau khi duoc buddy nhac lai
- Dat tu lam: vao ca la biet ngay viec uu tien cua minh
- Loi do: bo sot thong tin ca gay anh huong den van hanh

### 8.5. Item C1-05 - Giu ve sinh va an toan nen

- Muc dich: tao nen ky luat sach, gon, an toan ngay tu dau
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: biet lau diem ban, bo rac dung cho, giu tay sach, biet vat nao can tranh dat sai cho
- Tu danh gia: em con chua ro quy tac ve sinh nao
- Buddy quan sat gi: nhan vien co bo qua ve sinh khi bat dau ban khong
- Quan ly can kiem gi: khong de nguoi moi hoc theo thoi quen sai
- Chua dat: bo qua ve sinh can ban, can nhac nhieu lan
- Dat khi co kem: lam duoc khi buddy chi nhac truc tiep
- Dat tu lam: tu giu sach va nhan ra diem can lau / can xu ly
- Loi do: bo qua buoc ve sinh anh huong an toan thuc pham

### 8.6. Item C1-06 - Biet dung kenh thong tin va nhom chat

- Muc dich: khong rot thong tin va khong phai nhac tay qua nhieu
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: vao dung nhom chat, doc thong bao, biet cach phan hoi khi can
- Tu danh gia: em co tu mo nhom xem thong bao duoc chua
- Buddy quan sat gi: nhan vien co theo duoc thong tin hay bo sot
- Quan ly can kiem gi: nguoi moi da vao dung kenh chua
- Chua dat: khong vao duoc nhom, khong doc thong tin
- Dat khi co kem: vao duoc va doc khi duoc nhac
- Dat tu lam: tu theo doi thong tin can thiet cua ca
- Loi do: bo sot thong bao van hanh quan trong

## 9. Chuan item chi tiet - Chang 2A track thu ngan / phuc vu

### 9.1. Item TQ-01 - Chao khach va mo dau dung nhip

- Muc dich: tao diem vao quay ro, lich su va nhanh
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: chao khach, mo loi dung nhac, huong dan khach vao quy trinh order
- Tu danh gia: em co ngai mo loi voi khach khong
- Buddy quan sat gi: nhan vien co chu dong chao, co noi ro rang, co bi dung hinh
- Quan ly can kiem gi: mat truoc quay co giu duoc nhiet va lich su khong
- Chua dat: khong chu dong chao, noi qua nho hoac roi
- Dat khi co kem: chao duoc khi buddy dung gan va nhac nhac
- Dat tu lam: chu dong, ro rang, khong lam khach cho vo nghia
- Loi do: bo mac khach o dau quay

### 9.2. Item TQ-02 - Nhan order dung va xac nhan dung

- Muc dich: giam sai mon tu goc
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: nhan dung mon, size, duong, da, topping, nhac lai order cho khach khi can
- Tu danh gia: phan nao trong order em hay nham nhat
- Buddy quan sat gi: nhan vien co doc lai dung va hoi lai khi khong chac
- Quan ly can kiem gi: goc sai order co dang nam o dau quay hay khong
- Chua dat: bo sot size / topping / ghi sai order
- Dat khi co kem: nhan dung order khi buddy dung gan ho tro
- Dat tu lam: nhan va xac nhan dung lien tiep cac order co ban
- Loi do: sai order lap lai gay tra mon hoac lam lai

### 9.3. Item TQ-03 - Goi y ban them co ban ma khong lam cham dong khach

- Muc dich: giu chat luong phuc vu va doanh thu co ban
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: biet 1-2 mau goi y them do / topping / size dung ngu canh
- Tu danh gia: em co biet luc nao nen goi y, luc nao nen bo qua
- Buddy quan sat gi: goi y co tu nhien, co ngan gon, co lam dung luong phuc vu
- Quan ly can kiem gi: upsell khong lam mat nhip quay
- Chua dat: khong biet goi y hoac goi y roi, lam cham line
- Dat khi co kem: goi y duoc trong tinh huong de khi duoc nhac
- Dat tu lam: goi y ngan gon, dung luc, khong gay tac
- Loi do: goi y sai thong tin san pham gay sai order

### 9.4. Item TQ-04 - Xu ly thanh toan dung quy trinh

- Muc dich: tranh lech tien va tranh tac o quay order
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: thao tac dung quy trinh thanh toan duoc giao, doc dung tong tien, biet xac nhan trang thai thanh toan
- Tu danh gia: em co nham nhat cho nao trong luc thu tien
- Buddy quan sat gi: thao tac co binh tinh, co xac nhan lai voi khach
- Quan ly can kiem gi: khong tao loi co ban o khau thu tien
- Chua dat: thao tac nham, xac nhan sai, de line dung vi luong thu tien roi
- Dat khi co kem: lam duoc khi buddy dung ho tro sat
- Dat tu lam: xu ly tru cac giao dich co ban dung quy trinh
- Loi do: thu / xac nhan sai gay lech giao dich

### 9.5. Item TQ-05 - Ban giao order ro rang cho pha che

- Muc dich: giam nghen giua 2 dau quay
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: chuyen order ro, uu tien dung, bao cac diem dac biet neu co
- Tu danh gia: em co biet luc nao phai noi them cho dau pha che
- Buddy quan sat gi: co ban giao ngan gon, trung thong tin, khong om thong tin
- Quan ly can kiem gi: line order va line pha che noi nhau muot
- Chua dat: ban giao moi mo, thieu thong tin, gay nham mon
- Dat khi co kem: ban giao dung khi buddy nhac
- Dat tu lam: biet mon nao can nhac, nhac du ma gon
- Loi do: bo sot thong tin quan trong gay sai mon

### 9.6. Item TQ-06 - Giu mat truoc quay sach, gon, san sang tiep khach

- Muc dich: giu trai nghiem mat tien va tranh boi roi mat quay
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: giu quay order gon, bo ly / giay / rac dung cho, khong de mat quay lem nhem
- Tu danh gia: luc dong em co hay bo qua ve sinh mat quay khong
- Buddy quan sat gi: co giu sach trong luc phuc vu, hay doi den luc vang moi don
- Quan ly can kiem gi: mat quay co xuong chuan khi line tang khong
- Chua dat: de mat quay ban, bo do lung tung
- Dat khi co kem: don duoc khi buddy nhac
- Dat tu lam: tu nhan ra diem ban va xu ly nhanh khong tac line
- Loi do: mat quay ban / uot gay mat an toan cho khach va nhan vien

### 9.7. Item TQ-07 - Xu ly tinh huong co ban voi khach

- Muc dich: khong day moi vuong mac len quan ly ngay lap tuc
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: biet xin khach doi 1 chut, biet xin loi khi can, biet goi buddy / quan ly dung luc
- Tu danh gia: em ngai nhat tinh huong nao voi khach
- Buddy quan sat gi: nhan vien co dung binh tinh va biet gioi han cua minh
- Quan ly can kiem gi: vuong mac nho co duoc giu nhip o dau quay
- Chua dat: dung hinh, noi voi khach sai, tu xu ly qua tham quyen
- Dat khi co kem: xu ly duoc tinh huong de khi buddy dung sat
- Dat tu lam: xu ly duoc loi nho, biet escalte loi lon dung luc
- Loi do: thong tin sai cho khach hoac tranh cai voi khach

### 9.8. Item TQ-08 - Cuoi ca va ban giao mat truoc quay

- Muc dich: ca sau nhan lai quay gon, ro, du thong tin
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: don quang, bo sung do co ban, bao ton dong, ban giao viec do dang
- Tu danh gia: em co nho het cac viec cuoi ca cua dau quay khong
- Buddy quan sat gi: co bo sot viec co ban, co noi lai ro cho nguoi nhan ca sau
- Quan ly can kiem gi: dau quay sau ban giao co san sang tiep tuc hay khong
- Chua dat: bo sot viec cuoi ca, khong ban giao ro
- Dat khi co kem: hoan thanh duoc khi co checklist va buddy nhac
- Dat tu lam: tu khop dau viec va ban giao ro
- Loi do: bo sot viec cuoi ca gay anh huong ca sau

## 10. Chuan item chi tiet - Chang 2B track pha che

### 10.1. Item PC-01 - Chuan bi station dau ca

- Muc dich: dau pha che vao guong ma khong doi nho tung viec
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: set up dung cu, ly, nap, topping, khan, khu vuc thao tac va diem sach se can thiet
- Tu danh gia: em da nho du viec mo station chua
- Buddy quan sat gi: co bo sot do co ban, co set sai cho
- Quan ly can kiem gi: station mo ca co san sang phuc vu chua
- Chua dat: bo sot dung cu / nguyen lieu co ban
- Dat khi co kem: set duoc khi buddy diem tung dau viec
- Dat tu lam: tu mo station gon va du cho luong ca co ban
- Loi do: mo station thieu do gay nghen line ra mon

### 10.2. Item PC-02 - Doc dung ticket / order truoc khi lam

- Muc dich: tranh sai mon ngay dau day chuyen
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: doc dung mon, size, da, duong, topping, ghi chu dac biet
- Tu danh gia: em hay nham nhat thong tin nao trong ticket
- Buddy quan sat gi: nhan vien co doc lai ticket truoc khi thao tac
- Quan ly can kiem gi: loi sai mon co nam o dau doc ticket khong
- Chua dat: nham thong tin ticket, bo sot ghi chu
- Dat khi co kem: doc dung khi buddy nhac nhac
- Dat tu lam: doc ticket dung lien tiep truoc khi lam
- Loi do: sai ticket lap lai gay lam lai mon

### 10.3. Item PC-03 - Lam dung mon nen theo cong thuc

- Muc dich: giu chat luong ly nuoc dong deu
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: thao tac dung thu tu, dung dinh luong va dung nguyen lieu cho nhom mon nen da duoc giao
- Tu danh gia: em chua tu tin nhat nhom mon nao
- Buddy quan sat gi: dung cu dung, dinh luong dung, khong bo qua buoc quan trong
- Quan ly can kiem gi: chat luong ly nuoc co dung chuan nen khong
- Chua dat: sai cong thuc, sai thu tu, sai dinh luong
- Dat khi co kem: lam dung khi buddy dung kem tung mon
- Dat tu lam: lam dung on dinh cac mon nen duoc giao
- Loi do: sai cong thuc lap lai

### 10.4. Item PC-04 - Xu ly duong / da / topping dung yeu cau

- Muc dich: mon ra dung nhu order, giam tra mon
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: canh dung muc duong, da, topping theo order va quy tac cua quan
- Tu danh gia: em hay nham phan nao nhat trong duong da topping
- Buddy quan sat gi: co doi chieu lai ticket khong, co thao tac sach va gon khong
- Quan ly can kiem gi: ty le tra mon do sai customize co dang cao khong
- Chua dat: sai topping / sai muc da duong
- Dat khi co kem: lam dung khi buddy dung canh
- Dat tu lam: xu ly dung lien tiep cac case co ban
- Loi do: sai yeu cau dac biet lap lai

### 10.5. Item PC-05 - Dan tem, ra ly, chuyen mon dung

- Muc dich: mon dung nguoi, dung line, dung tay khach
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: doi ly dung ticket, dan tem neu co, dat mon vao line ra mon dung quy tac
- Tu danh gia: em co hay roi o buoc cuoi khong
- Buddy quan sat gi: co doi chieu lai ly va ticket truoc khi day mon
- Quan ly can kiem gi: loi o buoc cuoi co gay nhieu lam lai khong
- Chua dat: dan nham / dat nham / giao nham line
- Dat khi co kem: dung khi buddy nhac o buoc cuoi
- Dat tu lam: buoc cuoi gon, dung, it loi
- Loi do: giao nham ly cho line khach

### 10.6. Item PC-06 - Giu station sach va an toan trong luc lam

- Muc dich: giu nhip do va an toan thao tac
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: khong de station bay ban, uot, lem, dung cu dat sai cho, thung rac tran
- Tu danh gia: luc dong em bo qua diem sach nao nhieu nhat
- Buddy quan sat gi: nhan vien co vua lam vua giu station hay de toi luc vang moi don
- Quan ly can kiem gi: station co xuong chuan khi line tang khong
- Chua dat: de station roi, ban, uot, mat an toan
- Dat khi co kem: giu duoc khi buddy nhac
- Dat tu lam: tu giu station sach, an toan trong luong viec co ban
- Loi do: thao tac mat an toan thuc pham hoac an toan lao dong

### 10.7. Item PC-07 - Bao thieu nguyen lieu va loi dung luc

- Muc dich: tranh toi luc tac line moi bao
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: nhan ra topping / tra / sua / do dung sap het, bao dung nguoi truoc khi gay nghen
- Tu danh gia: em co de den luc het moi bao khong
- Buddy quan sat gi: nhan vien co de y muc ton va biet bao som
- Quan ly can kiem gi: nhan su moi co y thuc canh ton / canh loi chua
- Chua dat: de het do hoac co loi roi moi bao
- Dat khi co kem: bao duoc khi buddy nhac nho de y
- Dat tu lam: tu canh ton va bao som dung nguoi
- Loi do: de line nghen do khong bao loi / khong bao ton

### 10.8. Item PC-08 - Cuoi ca va ban giao station pha che

- Muc dich: station sau ca sach, du, ro, san sang
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: don station, bo sung thong tin ton, bao mon / topping / do dung can xu ly, giao lai viec do dang
- Tu danh gia: em co bo sot viec nao cuoi station khong
- Buddy quan sat gi: station co that su san sang cho ca sau khong
- Quan ly can kiem gi: station co duoc ban giao co chat luong hay khong
- Chua dat: bo sot don dep, khong bao ton, ban giao mo ho
- Dat khi co kem: lam duoc khi buddy checklist va nhac
- Dat tu lam: tu chot duoc station gon, sach, ro thong tin
- Loi do: ban giao station xuong chuan gay anh huong ca sau

## 11. Chuan item chi tiet - Chang 3 ca that co giam sat

Chang nay la chang "dung vi tri that trong ca that".
Moi item van tach tieu chuan theo vi tri.

### 11.1. Item C3-01 - Giu duoc nhip viec o vi tri chinh

- Ap dung cho thu ngan / phuc vu: giu duoc line order co ban, khong de khach cho vo nghia, khong bo khach o dau quay
- Ap dung cho pha che: giu duoc line mon co ban, khong roi thao tac, khong de mon dung vo nghia trong luong vua
- Tu danh gia: luc line len em hay rot o dau
- Buddy quan sat gi: nhan vien co bi dung hinh khi line tang nhe den vua
- Quan ly can kiem gi: da du tin de dung vi tri co giam sat chua
- Chua dat: mat nhip ro rang khi line tang nhe
- Dat khi co kem: giu duoc nhip neu buddy sat canh
- Dat tu lam: giu duoc nhip co ban trong ca that, buddy chi can quan sat
- Loi do: mat vi tri, bo line, gay tac van hanh

### 11.2. Item C3-02 - Phoi hop dung voi dau quay con lai

- Ap dung cho thu ngan / phuc vu: ban giao mon, thong tin va uu tien dung cho pha che
- Ap dung cho pha che: phan hoi dung thong tin, nhac lai diem bat thuong, dua mon ra line ro rang
- Tu danh gia: em co ngai goi ho tro hoac nhac lai thong tin khong
- Buddy quan sat gi: giao tiep co gon, ro, dung luc
- Quan ly can kiem gi: nghen co nam o ky nang phoi hop khong
- Chua dat: giao tiep roi, bo sot thong tin
- Dat khi co kem: phoi hop duoc khi buddy nhac
- Dat tu lam: phoi hop muot o cac case co ban
- Loi do: bo sot thong tin gay sai mon / sai line

### 11.3. Item C3-03 - Xu ly loi nho va bao loi dung luc

- Ap dung cho thu ngan / phuc vu: biet xin khach doi 1 chut, xin loi dung muc, goi ho tro khi vuot tham quyen
- Ap dung cho pha che: biet dung lai khi nghi sai mon, bao buddy / quan ly truoc khi day sai mon
- Tu danh gia: tinh huong nao em de co xu huong im lang nhat
- Buddy quan sat gi: co bao som hay giau loi
- Quan ly can kiem gi: thai do voi loi co an toan cho van hanh khong
- Chua dat: giau loi, bao muon, tu sua sai cach
- Dat khi co kem: bao duoc khi buddy goi mo
- Dat tu lam: thay loi la bao dung nguoi dung luc
- Loi do: giau loi gay anh huong khach / line / chat luong

### 11.4. Item C3-04 - Giu chuan sach va ky luat khi dang ban

- Ap dung cho thu ngan / phuc vu: mat quay gon, sach, khong tha rac va khong bo khach
- Ap dung cho pha che: station gon, sach, an toan du dang trong nhip vua
- Tu danh gia: luc ban em bo qua buoc nao nhieu nhat
- Buddy quan sat gi: co giu duoc ky luat hay "ban la bo qua het"
- Quan ly can kiem gi: chuan cua hang co duoc giu ngay ca luc ban
- Chua dat: line tang la mat sach, mat ky luat
- Dat khi co kem: giu duoc khi buddy nhac
- Dat tu lam: van giu chuan du ban muc co ban
- Loi do: xuong chuan ve sinh / an toan trong ca that

## 12. Chuan item chi tiet - Chang 4 tu chay ca co ban

Day la chang gate chat luong dau ra.
Quan ly phai co kha nang dua ra quyet dinh giao ca dua tren chang nay.

### 12.1. Item C4-01 - Tu vao guong dau ca

- Ap dung cho thu ngan / phuc vu: tu mo mat quay, kiem tra dung cu, biet uu tien dau ca
- Ap dung cho pha che: tu mo station, kiem tra ton co ban, vao line san sang
- Tu danh gia: em co con can nguoi nhac cac buoc dau ca khong
- Buddy quan sat gi: nhan vien co con phu thuoc vao checklist nhac tay qua nhieu khong
- Quan ly can kiem gi: co the giao vao ca ma khong can cam tay nua khong
- Chua dat: vao ca van cho giao tung viec nho
- Dat khi co kem: vao guong duoc khi buddy dung canh
- Dat tu lam: tu mo duoc vi tri va biet uu tien dau ca
- Loi do: vao ca mat nhip vi khong tu chuan bi duoc

### 12.2. Item C4-02 - Tu xu ly luong viec co ban on dinh

- Ap dung cho thu ngan / phuc vu: nhan line order co ban, xac nhan, chuyen mon va giu line on
- Ap dung cho pha che: xu ly nhom mon nen co ban lien tiep ma khong roi cong thuc
- Tu danh gia: muc line nao em thay minh van chua tu tin
- Buddy quan sat gi: chat luong co deu khi khong dung sat khong
- Quan ly can kiem gi: da du giao line / station co ban chua
- Chua dat: bo line, roi nhip, sai lap lai
- Dat khi co kem: xu ly duoc neu buddy chot sat
- Dat tu lam: xu ly on dinh line / station co ban
- Loi do: gay tac / sai lap lai trong luong co ban

### 12.3. Item C4-03 - Tu biet uu tien va goi ho tro dung luc

- Ap dung cho thu ngan / phuc vu: biet luc nao tiep khach, luc nao goi ho tro line, luc nao nho quan ly can thiep
- Ap dung cho pha che: biet luc nao tap trung line mon, luc nao bao sap het topping / can doi nguoi phu
- Tu danh gia: em co hay co gang tu om qua lau khong
- Buddy quan sat gi: co biet gioi han va escalte hop ly khong
- Quan ly can kiem gi: nhan vien co an toan de giao viec khong
- Chua dat: om viec qua kha nang, bao muon, gay tac
- Dat khi co kem: goi duoc ho tro khi buddy nhac
- Dat tu lam: tu biet diem can goi ho tro truoc khi nghen
- Loi do: khong goi ho tro dung luc gay anh huong ca

### 12.4. Item C4-04 - Tu dong ca va ban giao dat chuan

- Ap dung cho thu ngan / phuc vu: dong mat quay, thong tin ton dong, don dep, ban giao ro
- Ap dung cho pha che: dong station, bao ton, don station, ban giao viec do va diem can canh
- Tu danh gia: em co bo sot phan nao luc dong ca khong
- Buddy quan sat gi: dong ca co that su xong hay chi "co ve xong"
- Quan ly can kiem gi: ca sau co nhan lai duoc 1 diem xuat phat on khong
- Chua dat: dong ca sot viec, ban giao mo ho
- Dat khi co kem: dong duoc khi co checklist va buddy canh
- Dat tu lam: dong va ban giao dat chuan, ro, gon, du thong tin
- Loi do: de ton dong lon cho ca sau

### 12.5. Item C4-05 - Quan ly duyet giao ca co ban

- Muc dich: khoa quyet dinh dau ra
- Nhan vien phai lam gi: hoan tat chang va giai thich duoc diem manh / diem can canh cua minh
- Tu danh gia: em tu tin dung vi tri nay o muc nao
- Buddy quan sat gi: xac nhan nhan vien da on o muc nao, can canh diem gi neu giao ca
- Quan ly can kiem gi: co dam giao ca co ban chua, co can gan co theo doi 1-2 diem nao khong
- Chua dat: quan ly khong the dua ra quyet dinh giao ca
- Dat khi co kem: co the giao ca nhung van can sat canh
- Dat tu lam: co the giao ca co ban voi muc canh thong thuong
- Loi do: quan ly chua du tin nhung he thong van cho qua

## 14. Cach hien tren 3 mat UI

### 14.1. Mat nhan vien

Chi hien ro 4 dieu:

- hom nay can lam item nao
- item nao can lam lai
- ai dang giu buoc tiep theo
- em dang o muc `can kem` hay `tu lam duoc`

Nhan vien khong can thay qua nhieu so lieu tong hop.

### 14.2. Mat buddy

Can thay nhanh:

- ai dang cho minh danh gia
- ai dang bi tra ve nhieu lan
- item nao dang o muc `dat khi co kem` chua len duoc `dat tu lam`

### 14.3. Mat quan ly

Can thay nhanh:

- nhan su nao da san sang giao ca
- nhan su nao van do o item gate
- buddy nao dang la diem nghen
- loi do nao lap lai nhieu nhat theo vi tri

## 15. Pham vi khong lam trong tai lieu nay

Tai lieu nay chua chot:

- full track hoc cheo giua 2 vi tri sau khi da qua onboarding
- rule len `shift leader`
- chuan danh gia KPI dai han
- scheduler nhac viec that theo gio / ngay
- lien thong voi payroll hoac xet thuong

## 16. Verify cho pass code sau spec

Khi code theo tai lieu nay, can verify it nhat:

1. nhan vien moi vao dung track `thu ngan / phuc vu` thi khong thay item `pha che` la item bat buoc
2. nhan vien moi vao dung track `pha che` thi khong thay item `thu ngan / phuc vu` la item bat buoc
3. buddy co the cham ket qua `dat_khi_co_kem`, `dat_tu_lam`, `can_kem_lai`
4. quan ly chi phai duyet o chang gate, khong phai cham tung item nho
5. he thong khong mo chang 4 neu con `loi do`
6. man quan ly doc 1 lan la biet ai `san sang giao ca`, ai `chua san sang`
7. man nhan vien doc 1 lan la biet minh dang o muc `can kem` hay `tu lam`
