# Onboarding 3 mat theo chang linh hoat

Ngay: `2026-05-28`
Pham vi: onboarding van hanh cho `nhan vien moi`, `buddy`, `quan ly`
Trang thai: `approved-for-spec`

## 1. Muc tieu

Chot huong onboarding theo `chang linh hoat`, de he thong tu mo chang dua tren tien do va dieu kien thuc te, khong ep theo so ngay cung.

Huong nay phai dat 4 ket qua:

- nhan vien moi mo vao la biet `hom nay lam gi`
- buddy mo vao la biet `phai kem ai, cham gi`
- quan ly mo vao la biet `ai dang block, lech o dau`
- he thong tu xac dinh `chang hien tai`, `blocker`, `nguoi dang no hanh dong`

Muc tieu cua pass nay la chot flow nghiep vu va wireframe chu de sau do giao sang plan code, chua doi backend that.

## 2. Vi sao khong dung checklist phang nua

Checklist phang hop cho demo nhanh, nhung khong du suc cho van hanh F&B khi co nhieu vai tro cung tham gia.

Neu giu checklist phang, se gap 4 van de:

- nhan vien khong ro viec nao quan trong nhat hom nay
- buddy de sot viec cho danh gia va kem cap
- quan ly thay du lieu roi rac, kho biet ai dang gay tac
- he thong khong phan biet duoc `da tick` voi `da dat`

Vi vay onboarding phai doi thanh `plan theo chang`.

## 3. Huong da chot

Huong duoc chon la `Onboarding van hanh 3 mat`.

Day la huong can bang nhat cho giai doan hien tai:

- du thuc chien de dua vao van hanh
- khong bien app thanh workflow engine nang
- de gan them rule he thong o pass sau
- co the rollout theo pass nho

Khong chon:

- `Checklist nang cap nhe` vi van qua phang, yeu o goc buddy va quan ly
- `Workflow engine day du` vi nang scope, de lam app rom ngay tu dau

## 4. Nguyen tac loi

Onboarding khong quyet dinh bang `da qua bao nhieu ngay`.
Onboarding quyet dinh bang `muc do hoan thanh thuc te` va `dieu kien van hanh`.

Cong thuc loi:

- du dieu kien thi mo chang
- chua du thi chan
- bi ket thi canh bao
- cho lau thi nhac dung nguoi

Muc tieu cua rule la phan anh dung trang thai that ngoai quan, khong chi ghi nhan da tick hay chua.

## 5. Cau truc nghiep vu loi

Moi nhan vien co 1 onboarding plan theo chang.

Moi chang co:

- muc tieu chang
- checklist phai lam
- checklist phai dat
- nguoi chiu trach nhiem chinh
- dieu kien qua chang sau
- tin hieu canh bao neu bi ket

Moi item khong chi co `done/chua done`, ma co 5 trang thai:

- `chua lam`
- `dang hoc`
- `dat`
- `can kem them`
- `khong ap dung`

Moi item phai hien du 4 lop:

- nhan vien phai lam gi
- buddy phai huong dan gi
- quan ly can kiem gi
- tieu chuan dat la gi

## 6. Rule he thong

### 6.1. Rule chang hien tai

Moi nhan vien chi co 1 `chang hien tai`.

Cach xac dinh:

- chang dau tien chua pass hoan toan se la chang hien tai
- neu chang hien tai da du dieu kien bat buoc thi moi xet mo chang sau
- neu chua du dieu kien thi van giu o chang hien tai

### 6.2. Rule mo chang tiep

Moi chang co 2 lop dieu kien:

- `dieu kien bat buoc`
- `dieu kien ho tro`

Quy tac:

- dieu kien bat buoc phai dat thi moi mo chang tiep
- dieu kien ho tro chua du thi van co the mo, nhung phai gan co theo doi

Vi du dieu kien bat buoc:

- tat ca item bat buoc da `dat`
- khong con item bat buoc o trang thai `can kem them`
- khong con item bat buoc cho danh gia qua lau ma chua co quyet dinh

Vi du dieu kien ho tro:

- da xong da so item nen hoan thanh
- da co it nhat 1 lan buddy xac nhan tien bo
- khong co canh bao lech chuan moi phat sinh

### 6.3. Rule pass chang

Mot chang duoc xem la `pass` khi:

- toan bo item bat buoc da `dat` hoac `khong ap dung` hop le
- khong con blocker mo
- neu chang can quan ly duyet cuoi thi phai co xac nhan quan ly

`Khong ap dung` chi hop le khi co ly do ro, khong duoc dung de lach chang.

### 6.4. Rule blocker

Blocker la thu dang chan nhan vien qua chang.

He thong tu gan blocker theo 3 nhom:

- `block do item`
- `block do nguoi xu ly`
- `block do rule chang`

Block do item:

- item bat buoc chua dat
- item bat buoc dang `can kem them`

Block do nguoi xu ly:

- nhan vien chua xong buoc cua minh
- buddy chua danh gia
- quan ly chua duyet

Block do rule chang:

- con thieu dieu kien mo chang sau
- con co rui ro can quan ly xem

### 6.5. Rule nguoi dang no hanh dong

He thong phai luon suy ra `action owner`:

- item `chua lam` hoac `dang hoc` -> nhan vien dang no hanh dong
- item `cho danh gia` -> buddy dang no hanh dong
- item `cho duyet cuoi` -> quan ly dang no hanh dong
- item `can kem them` -> nhan vien lam lai, buddy van gan trach nhiem kem

### 6.6. Rule nhac viec va canh bao

Nhac viec:

- chi nhac dung nguoi dang no hanh dong
- noi ro viec gi dang chan cai gi

Canh bao:

- item bat buoc o lau ma khong doi trang thai
- cho danh gia qua lau
- bi tra ve nhieu lan
- nhieu nguoi cung ket 1 item
- chang sau chua mo duoc vi 1 diem nghen keo dai

Muc canh bao:

- `can chu y`
- `dang cham`
- `co nguy co lech chuan`

### 6.7. Rule ngoai le va dau vet

He thong cho phep ngoai le, nhung phai co kiem soat:

- item `khong ap dung`
- doi buddy giua chung
- quan ly cho qua co dieu kien

Moi ngoai le phai luu:

- ai quyet dinh
- ly do ngan
- anh huong toi pass chang

Toi thieu phai luu dau vet:

- ai doi trang thai
- doi tu gi sang gi
- luc nao doi
- neu tra ve hoac ngoai le thi vi sao

## 7. Goc nhin 3 vai tro

### 7.1. Nhan vien moi

Nhan vien moi can thay:

- dang o chang nao
- hom nay can lam gi
- dang cho ai
- con thieu gi de qua chang

Man nhan vien la `man dinh huong hanh dong hang ngay`, khong phai man doc quy trinh.

### 7.2. Buddy

Buddy can thay:

- hom nay phai kem ai
- viec nao cho minh danh gia
- ai dang ket vi minh chua xu ly
- ai co dau hieu cham hoac lech chuan

Man buddy la `man hang doi kem cap va danh gia`, khong phai man quan tri tong.

### 7.3. Quan ly

Quan ly can thay:

- tong quan onboarding cua hang
- ai dang block
- buddy nao dang nghen
- chang nao co ty le cham hoac tra ve cao
- viec nao can can thiep

Man quan ly la `man phat hien rui ro va dieu phoi can thiep`, khong phai man xem checklist chi tiet.

## 8. Wireframe chu

### 8.1. Wireframe chu cho nhan vien moi

#### Man 1 - Tong quan onboarding

- Header: `Onboarding cua ban`
- Card `Chang hien tai`
  - ten chang
  - muc tieu chang
  - trang thai tong quan: `dang hoc / gan dat / dang bi chan`
- Card `Hom nay can lam gi`
  - danh sach 3-5 viec uu tien
  - moi viec co: ten viec, trang thai, ai se danh gia
- Card `Dang cho ai`
  - `1 viec dang cho buddy danh gia`
  - `1 viec dang cho quan ly xac nhan`
- Card `Con thieu gi de qua chang`
  - `con 2 viec bat buoc chua dat`
  - `1 viec can kem them`

#### Man 2 - Chi tiet 1 item

- ten item
- vi sao item nay quan trong
- `Ban can lam gi`
- `Tieu chuan dat`
- `Nguoi se danh gia`
- `Trang thai hien tai`
- nut hanh dong:
  - `Bat dau hoc`
  - `Danh dau da luyen xong`
  - `Yeu cau danh gia`
  - `Xem gop y`
  - `Xem lai huong dan`

#### Man 3 - Gop y va lam lai

- Header: `Can kem them`
- noi dung gop y cua buddy
- diem chua dat
- viec can lam lai
- nut:
  - `Da luyen lai`
  - `Yeu cau danh gia lai`

### 8.2. Wireframe chu cho buddy

#### Man 1 - Hang doi kem cap

- Header: `Viec buddy can xu ly`
- Tab `Can huong dan`
- Tab `Can quan sat`
- Tab `Cho danh gia`
- Tab `Can kem lai`

Moi the nhan vien:

- ten nhan vien
- chang hien tai
- muc tieu chang
- so viec bat buoc con thieu
- canh bao neu dang ket lau
- nhan `Dang cho ban`

#### Man 2 - Chi tiet nhan vien

- Khoi `Tong quan`
  - chang hien tai
  - so item dat
  - so item can kem them
  - blocker hien tai
- Khoi `Viec cho ban xu ly`
  - danh sach item cho danh gia
- Khoi `Nguy co cham`
  - cho danh gia lau
  - tra ve nhieu lan

#### Man 3 - Cham 1 item

- ten item
- `Nhan vien phai lam gi`
- `Buddy can huong dan gi`
- `Quan ly se kiem gi`
- `Tieu chuan dat`
- nut:
  - `Da huong dan`
  - `Da quan sat`
  - `Danh gia dat`
  - `Yeu cau luyen them`
- neu tra ve:
  - o bat buoc `Gop y cu the`

### 8.3. Wireframe chu cho quan ly

#### Man 1 - Tong quan onboarding cua hang

- Header: `Onboarding cua hang`
- Card so:
  - `Tong nhan vien dang onboarding`
  - `Dang di dung huong`
  - `Dang block`
  - `Can can thiep ngay`
- Khoi `Nhan vien can chu y`
  - ten
  - chang hien tai
  - ly do block
  - dang cho ai
  - muc uu tien
- Khoi `Buddy dang nghen`
  - ten buddy
  - so viec cho danh gia
  - so nhan vien dang bi ket vi buddy
- Khoi `Chang hoac item co van de`
  - chang nao tac nhieu
  - item nao bi tra ve cao

#### Man 2 - Chi tiet ca block

- ten nhan vien
- chang hien tai
- cac blocker dang mo
- ai dang no hanh dong
- lich su gop y ngan
- hanh dong quan ly:
  - `Nhac buddy`
  - `Vao danh gia truc tiep`
  - `Doi buddy`
  - `Cho qua ngoai le`
  - `Giu chua mo chang tiep`

#### Man 3 - Goc nhin theo chang

- danh sach cac chang
- moi chang co:
  - so nguoi dang o chang
  - so nguoi block
  - item truot nhieu nhat
  - buddy lien quan

Muc tieu la thay van de o thiet ke dao tao, khong chi o tung nguoi.

## 9. Pham vi rollout de xuat

Nen chia rollout theo 4 pass:

### Pass A - Nen du lieu va rule loi

- dinh nghia model chang, item, status, blocker
- service tinh `chang hien tai`, `action owner`, `blocker`
- mock data cho 3 vai tro

### Pass B - Man nhan vien moi

- man tong quan
- item detail
- luong `dang hoc -> cho danh gia -> can kem them / dat`

### Pass C - Man buddy

- hang doi kem cap
- cham item
- gop y bat buoc khi tra ve

### Pass D - Man quan ly

- tong quan cua hang
- danh sach block
- canh bao va hanh dong can thiep

## 10. Ngoai pham vi pass nay

Chua lam trong pham vi spec nay:

- workflow engine day du kieu BPM
- escalation nhieu tang phuc tap
- audit log day du cap doanh nghiep lon
- backend that, migration that, notification push that

## 11. Tieu chi xem la dung huong

Huong nay duoc xem la dung khi:

- nhan vien mo vao biet ngay viec uu tien trong ngay
- buddy thay ro ai dang cho minh
- quan ly thay ro ai dang gay tac va can nhay vao dau
- he thong co the giai thich `dang ket vi cai gi, dang cho ai`

## 12. Ket luan chot

Onboarding can duoc xem la flow van hanh theo chang co dieu kien, khong phai danh sach tick cho xong.

Ban spec nay la nen de sang buoc ke tiep:

- viet implementation plan
- handoff cho AI Code theo pass nho
