# HDSD Flow KPI Co Huong Dan Cho CEO Va HR Admin

Ngay lap: 2026-08-25  
Trang thai: Cho nguoi dung duyet  
Pham vi: Tu thiet lap KPI lan dau den van hanh hang thang, khoa ky va xet tang bac

## 1. Muc dich tai lieu

Tai lieu nay mo ta cach CEO va HR Admin su dung khu vuc `KPI & Phat trien` theo mot hanh trinh lien tuc. Nguoi dung khong can nho route, khong can tu doan trang nao phai vao truoc.

Moi thoi diem, giao dien phai tra loi duoc 5 cau hoi:

1. Ky KPI dang o giai doan nao?
2. Ai la nguoi can thuc hien buoc hien tai?
3. Viec gi dang thieu hoac dang chan?
4. Nut chinh can bam la gi?
5. Hoan thanh xong se chuyen den buoc nao?

## 2. Doi tuong su dung

### CEO

- Duyet va ap dung chuong trinh KPI.
- Theo doi tinh hinh KPI toan chuoi.
- Quyet dinh cong bo ket qua.
- Xu ly khieu nai thuoc tham quyen.
- Khoa ky KPI.
- Duyet ho so tang bac va cac de xuat luong lien quan.

### HR Admin

- Tao va chuan bi chuong trinh KPI.
- Mo ky KPI theo thang, cua hang va nhom nhan vien.
- Theo doi tien do danh gia.
- Don dep du lieu thieu, loi chan va ho so bat thuong.
- Chuan bi bo ho so de CEO ra quyet dinh.

## 3. Nguyen tac giao dien bat buoc

- CEO va HR Admin luon bat dau tai `Tong quan KPI`.
- Moi thoi diem chi co mot nut hanh dong chinh.
- Luon ghi ro nhan `CEO can lam`, `HR Admin can lam` hoac `Dang cho vai tro khac`.
- Khong dua nguoi dung thang vao mot man hinh phuc tap ma khong co phan giai thich ngan.
- Khong cho cong bo, khoa ky hoac duyệt tang bac khi con loi chan.
- Hoan thanh mot buoc thi quay lai `Tong quan KPI` va tu dong chon buoc tiep theo.
- Cac trang chuyen sau van ton tai, nhung duoc mo tu dung ngu canh thay vi day het ra menu trai.

## 4. Ban do 5 muc KPI

| Muc menu | Cau hoi ma trang phai tra loi | Nguoi dung chinh |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tong quan KPI | Hom nay toi can lam gi tiep theo? | CEO, HR Admin |
| Viec can danh gia | Ai dang cham, ai cham thieu, ai qua han? | HR theo doi; quan ly thuc hien |
| Ket qua & cai thien | Ket qua da cong bo la gi, ai can cai thien? | CEO, HR, quan ly, nhan vien theo quyen |
| San sang tang bac | Ai du dieu kien, ai con thieu gi, ai dang bi chan? | HR chuan bi, CEO duyet |
| Chuong trinh danh gia | KPI dung de lam gi, ap dung cho ai va tinh nhu the nao? | HR tao, CEO duyet |

### Cac phan lien quan nam ngoai menu KPI

| Phan lien quan | Vi tri de vao | Quan he voi KPI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thuong BSC cua hang | Luong, thuong & tam ung | Dung ket qua van hanh/cua hang de tinh thuong, khong phai noi thiet lap KPI nhan su |
| Su co & Vi pham | Van hanh & Noi bo | Tao bang chung va tac dong den danh gia, ky luat hoac dieu kien tang bac |
| Bao cao KPI | Bao cao & Phan tich | Xem xu huong, rui ro, pipeline va bang xep hang theo quyen |

## 5. Flow tong the

`Thiet lap -> Duyet chuong trinh -> Mo ky -> Danh gia -> Kiem tra -> Cong bo -> Khieu nai -> Khoa ky -> Tang bac`

| Buoc | Nguoi chiu trach nhiem | Ket qua dau ra |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Thiet lap chuong trinh | HR Admin | Co ban nhap day du |
| 2. Duyet chuong trinh | CEO | Co chuong trinh dang ap dung |
| 3. Mo ky KPI | HR Admin | Co ky KPI dung thang, cua hang va nhan vien |
| 4. Danh gia | Quan ly/leader thuc hien, HR theo doi | Tat ca phieu hop le va da gui |
| 5. Kiem tra truoc cong bo | HR chuan bi | Khong con du lieu thieu hoac loi chan |
| 6. Cong bo ket qua | CEO | Nhan vien xem duoc ket qua, bat dau thoi han khieu nai |
| 7. Xu ly khieu nai | CEO quyet dinh, HR ho tro | Moi ho so co ket luan |
| 8. Khoa ky | CEO | Du lieu thang duoc dong va khong con sua |
| 9. Xet tang bac | HR chuan bi, CEO duyet | Co quyet dinh hoac ke hoach bo sung dieu kien |

## 6. HDSD CEO - Lan dau su dung

### Buoc 1 - Mo Tong quan KPI

CEO chon `KPI & Phat trien -> Tong quan KPI`.

Neu chua co chuong trinh dang ap dung, giao dien hien:

> Chua co chuong trinh KPI dang ap dung  
> HR Admin can tao bo tieu chi va pham vi ap dung. CEO se kiem tra truoc khi phat hanh.  
> Nut chinh: `Xem quy trinh thiet lap`

Nut nay mo `Chuong trinh danh gia` o che do xem va ghi ro buoc hien tai do HR Admin thuc hien. CEO khong bi dan vao form chinh sua.

### Buoc 2 - Theo doi HR chuan bi chuong trinh

Neu HR dang tao ban nhap, CEO thay:

> HR Admin dang chuan bi chuong trinh  
> Da hoan thanh 3/5 buoc. Con thieu nguon danh gia va dieu kien tang bac.  
> Nut chinh: `Xem tien do thiet lap`

CEO co the xem, nhung giao dien phai ghi ro noi dung nao dang la ban nhap va chua co hieu luc.

### Buoc 3 - Kiem tra va duyệt chuong trinh

Khi HR hoan thanh, CEO thay mot the `Chuong trinh cho duyet`.

CEO can kiem tra:

- Muc tieu chinh cua chuong trinh.
- Cua hang va chuc danh ap dung.
- Nguon danh gia duoc su dung.
- Ty trong cac nhom noi dung.
- Dieu kien san sang tang bac.
- Cac ngoai le theo cua hang hoac chuc danh.
- Ngay bat dau ap dung.

Nut chinh: `Duyet & ap dung`.

Neu con loi chan, nut duyet bi khoa va giao dien hien ro HR can sua noi dung nao.

### Buoc 4 - Chuyen sang mo ky KPI

Sau khi duyệt, he thong dua CEO ve Tong quan KPI.

Giao dien hien:

> Chuong trinh da san sang  
> HR Admin can mo ky KPI cho thang tiep theo.  
> Nut chinh: `Xem trang thai mo ky`

CEO khong can tu tao tung phieu danh gia.

## 7. HDSD HR Admin - Thiet lap lan dau

### Buoc 1 - Chon muc tieu

HR vao `Chuong trinh danh gia` va chon muc tieu, vi du:

- Danh gia va cai thien hang thang.
- Xet thuong.
- Xet san sang tang bac.
- Danh gia ky luat va chat luong van hanh.

Giao dien de xuat bo mau Homies thay vi bat HR tu viet moi.

### Buoc 2 - Chon pham vi

HR chon:

- Cua hang ap dung.
- Chuc danh/cap bac ap dung.
- Thoi gian ap dung.
- Lo trinh tang bac lien quan.

### Buoc 3 - Xac nhan nguon danh gia

HR kiem tra cac nguon du lieu nhu doanh thu, cham cong, dao tao, van hanh, su co va danh gia cua quan ly.

Moi nguon phai hien mot trong cac trang thai:

- San sang.
- Can xac nhan thu cong.
- Chua ket noi.
- Khong ap dung.

### Buoc 4 - Xac nhan dieu kien tang bac

HR kiem tra cac dieu kien nhu KPI lien tiep, thoi gian o cap bac, gio cong hop le, bai test, challenge va vi pham dang chan.

### Buoc 5 - Xem truoc va gui CEO duyệt

Man xem truoc phai cho biet:

- Chuong trinh ap dung cho bao nhieu nhan vien.
- Co bao nhieu cua hang/chuc danh.
- Noi dung nao con thieu.
- Co loi chan hay chi co canh bao.
- Khi phat hanh se co hieu luc tu ngay nao.

Nut chinh: `Gui CEO duyệt`.

## 8. HDSD van hanh KPI hang thang

### Giai doan 1 - HR mo ky KPI

Tai Tong quan KPI, HR thay:

> Chua mo ky KPI thang nay  
> Chuong trinh dang ap dung: [Ten chuong trinh]  
> Nut chinh: `Kiem tra roi mo ky`

Truoc khi mo, HR xac nhan:

- Thang danh gia.
- Cua hang ap dung.
- Danh sach nhan vien.
- Chuong trinh/phien ban duoc su dung.
- Moc han cham va han quan ly xac nhan.

### Giai doan 2 - Theo doi danh gia

HR khong can mo tung phieu neu khong co bat thuong. Tong quan phai hien:

- Phan tram da hoan thanh.
- So phieu chua bat dau.
- So phieu qua han.
- Nguon du lieu con thieu.
- Cua hang can ho tro.
- Co liem chinh hoac canh bao bat thuong.

Nut chinh thay doi theo tinh hinh, vi du `Nhac 8 phieu qua han` hoac `Xu ly 3 loi du lieu`.

### Giai doan 3 - HR kiem tra truoc cong bo

Khi tat ca nguoi cham da gui, HR thay checklist:

- Tat ca nhan vien co phieu.
- Khong thieu diem bat buoc.
- Dieu chinh diem co ly do.
- Diem thap co bang chung theo quy dinh.
- Cac co bat thuong da duoc xu ly hoac ghi nhan.
- Khong con ho so o trang thai ban nhap/bi tra lai.

Dat du dieu kien, HR bam `Gui CEO cong bo`.

### Giai doan 4 - CEO cong bo ket qua

CEO thay:

> Ky KPI da san sang cong bo  
> Hoan thanh: 100%  
> Loi chan: 0  
> Canh bao da ghi nhan: [so luong]  
> Nut chinh: `Kiem tra & cong bo ket qua`

Truoc khi xac nhan, giao dien nhac:

- Nhan vien se xem duoc ket qua.
- Cua so khieu nai 48 gio se bat dau.
- Ket qua cong bo phai dung dung phien ban KPI cua ky.

### Giai doan 5 - CEO xu ly khieu nai

Trong 48 gio, Tong quan KPI hien:

- Thoi gian con lai.
- So ho so chua xu ly.
- Muc do uu tien.
- Cac ho so co tac dong den tang bac, ky luat hoac luong.

Moi ho so can hien bang chung hai ben va bat buoc CEO ghi ket luan.

### Giai doan 6 - CEO khoa ky

Chi hien nut `Khoa ky KPI` khi:

- Da het cua so khieu nai.
- Khong con ho so chua ket luan.
- Khong con thao tac dieu chinh dang cho.

Sau khi khoa, du lieu thang chi duoc xem, khong duoc sua.

### Giai doan 7 - HR va CEO xet tang bac

Sau khi khoa ky, he thong tu dua nhan vien vao ba nhom:

- Chua du dieu kien.
- Sap du dieu kien.
- San sang duoc xet.

HR chuan bi ho so, test, challenge va de xuat. CEO xem toan bo ly do dat/chua dat truoc khi quyet dinh.

## 9. Khoi `Viec can lam tiep theo`

Khoi nay nam ngay dau Tong quan KPI va gom:

1. Nhan vai tro dang chiu trach nhiem.
2. Tieu de viec can lam.
3. Mot cau giai thich vi sao viec nay xuat hien.
4. Tien do hoac thoi han.
5. Danh sach dieu kien dat/chua dat.
6. Mot nut hanh dong chinh.
7. Mot lien ket phu `Xem chi tiet`.

Vi du CEO:

> CEO can cong bo ket qua thang 08/2026  
> HR da hoan tat doi soat 4 cua hang. Khong con loi chan.  
> 48 nhan vien se nhan ket qua sau khi cong bo.  
> Nut chinh: `Kiem tra & cong bo`

Vi du HR:

> HR Admin can xu ly du lieu thieu  
> Con 6 nhan vien chua co du lieu cham cong hop le.  
> Nut chinh: `Mo danh sach can xu ly`

## 11. Xu ly truong hop dac biet

### Khong co du lieu

Khong hien so 0 nhu mot ket qua xau. Hien ro `Chua co du lieu` va noi ai can bo sung.

### Nguoi dung khong co quyen

Khong hien nut hanh dong. Hien `Buoc nay do [vai tro] thuc hien` va cho phep xem trang thai neu duoc phep.

### Co loi chan

Nut chinh doi thanh `Xu ly loi dang chan`. Moi loi phai ghi ro noi xu ly va nguoi chiu trach nhiem.

### Co canh bao nhung van duoc tiep tuc

Tach ro `Canh bao` va `Loi chan`. Neu CEO van tiep tuc, bat buoc ghi nhan ly do khi nghiep vu yeu cau.

### Du lieu da khoa

Chi cho xem. Neu can sua chinh sach, tao phien ban chuong trinh moi; khong sua lai ket qua lich su.

## 12. RPM Canvas

- RESULT: CEO/HR tim duoc viec can lam tiep theo trong toi da 10 giay va hoan thanh flow tu thiet lap den khoa ky ma khong can nho route.
- HARD RESULT: Nguoi moi co the tao chuong trinh mau trong 10 phut, van hanh ky thang theo mot CTA chinh va khong thuc hien sai thu tu.
- PURPOSE: Giam phu thuoc vao nguoi biet he thong, giam sai sot khi cong bo/khoa ky va lam ro trach nhiem CEO - HR.
- FMA: Neu chon sai buoc, co the mo ky sai phien ban, cong bo khi thieu du lieu, bo sot khieu nai hoac dung ket qua chua khoa de xet tang bac.

## 14. Pham vi thiet ke giao dien de code sau khi duyet

### Trong pham vi

- Them stepper hanh trinh KPI tren Tong quan KPI.
- Them khoi `Viec can lam tiep theo` theo vai tro va trang thai.
- Them bang huong dan ngan truoc khi mo man chuyen sau.
- Dieu huong CTA den cac trang hien co.
- Hien ly do bi chan, nguoi can xu ly va buoc tiep theo.
- Them test cho bang quyet dinh CTA cua CEO va HR.

### Ngoai pham vi

- Khong doi cong thuc tinh KPI.
- Khong migration database.
- Khong doi quyen truy cap.
- Khong xoa/doi route.
- Khong lam lai giao dien tat ca trang KPI trong cung mot pass.

## 15. Tieu chi nghiem thu

- CEO chua co chuong trinh thay ro HR la nguoi thiet lap va co CTA xem quy trinh, khong hien form chinh sua.
- HR chua co chuong trinh thay CTA bat dau thiet lap KPI.
- CEO co ban cho duyet thay CTA kiem tra va duyệt.
- HR co chuong trinh da duyệt nhung chua co ky thay CTA mo ky.
- HR thay dung tien do, qua han va loi chan khi ky dang danh gia.
- CEO chi thay CTA cong bo khi khong con loi chan.
- CEO thay queue khieu nai va thoi han sau khi cong bo.
- CEO chi khoa duoc ky khi khong con ho so mo.
- Ky da khoa dan den ho so tang bac, khong quay lai sua diem.
- Moi CTA mo dung trang va quay ve Tong quan KPI sau khi hoan thanh.
- Giao dien desktop va mobile deu doc duoc flow.

## 16. Rollback

- Giu nguyen cac trang va route KPI hien tai.
- Khoi huong dan chi la lop dieu huong moi tren Tong quan KPI.
- Neu lop huong dan loi, co the an khoi nay va nguoi dung van vao 5 menu KPI nhu hien tai.
- Khong co du lieu KPI nao bi xoa hoac chuyen doi khi rollback.
